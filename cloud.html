Installation and Services

We offer customized design and professional installation of high-definition surveillance systems for businesses, residential properties, transportation vehicles, and public spaces. Our solutions ensure enhanced security, reliability, and seamless integration tailored to specific client needs.

Remote Monitoring

We enable secure remote access to surveillance systems, allowing users to monitor security footage in real time via desktops and mobile devices. Our solutions ensure convenient, reliable, and seamless connectivity from anywhere

Maintenance and Upgrades

We provide Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) and Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (CAMC) for existing systems, along with system upgrades based on evolving client requirements to ensure seamless surveillance operations. Our services include camera performance and visibility checks, NVR recording verification, configuration review, and disk health diagnostics. Coverage under AMC and CAMC also includes the replacement of CCTV components and select consumable items, ensuring consistent system reliability and performance.

Types of Cameras

We specialize in a wide range of surveillance solutions, including IP and analog cameras with both wired and wireless configurations. Our portfolio includes multi-sensor panoramic cameras, PTZ (Pan-Tilt-Zoom), thermal imaging, and fisheye lens technologies. These systems are seamlessly integrated with access control and intrusion detection systems, featuring automated alerts and AI-based anomaly detection. Our solutions are supported by advanced Video Management Systems (VMS) and user-friendly applications for efficient monitoring and control.

Access Control Systems

We provide seamless integration with advanced access control solutions, including smart locks, keycard entry systems, biometric attendance devices, RFID readers, and boom barrier systems with Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR). Our solutions are designed to enhance security, streamline access management, and integrate efficiently with existing infrastructure.
